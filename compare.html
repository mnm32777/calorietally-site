Switching over

Five minutes to your first meal

Questions

Before you switch

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free to download, has no ads, and works at no cost with a free AI key. Premium is optional, for people who want zero setup and the extras.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Your meals, weigh-ins and recipes stay on your phone, and you can back them up yourself.

How accurate is it?

Everyday foods are checked against the USDA's lab-measured database, packaged foods use the printed label, and chain restaurants use their published numbers. Anything estimated says so.

Android and iPhone?

Both. Android 8 or newer, or an iPhone with iOS 17 or newer.
