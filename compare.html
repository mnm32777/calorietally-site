In their own policies

What they say about your data

MyFitnessPal

  • Its privacy policy says it shares your information with marketing and advertising partners and engages in targeted advertising.
  • It says some of its cookie uses may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of data under state privacy laws.
  • In March 2026 it announced an advertising business built on its users' nutrition and fitness data (an opt-in data feed, AdExchanger reported).
  • In 2018 a breach exposed about 150 million accounts.
Privacy policy · Google Play · AdExchanger · CBS News

Lose It!

  • Its Google Play listing says it contains ads and shares personal info and device IDs with other companies for advertising or marketing.
  • The same listing says it collects the list of apps installed on your phone.
Google Play

Cronometer

  • Says it does not sell personal information. For free users, it shares limited information with ad networks to show targeted ads, but not health information.
  • Ads go away with Gold, $59.99 a year.
