Privacy policy

Your food log is yours

Effective October 18, 2026. AteRight is made by Cardrama, LLC. This page explains, in plain words, what data the app touches and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your food log, recipes, weigh-ins, and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no AteRight account and no AteRight cloud copy.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text or photo for that request is sent to an AI service to be processed, and is not stored by us. The app asks your permission first, naming the service.
  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking across apps or websites. We never sell data.

Data on your device

Meals you log, your saved foods and recipes, weigh-ins, your goal and body details (height, weight, birth year, activity level), and your preferences are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. AteRight makes no backups of its own: a backup file exists only if you make one with "Export backup", and you choose where that file goes. If your phone's own backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the phone may include the app's data in that backup; that copy belongs to your Google or Apple account, not to us, and we cannot see it. Your AI keys and license key are kept encrypted in the phone's secure storage.

AI features

To work out calories, read photos, analyze recipes, or answer chat questions, the app sends that request's content (for example "turkey sandwich and an apple", or a photo of your plate, a label, a menu, or a recipe) to an AI service:

  • With your own key (free version): directly to the provider you chose (Groq, Google Gemini, or Anthropic), under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Your key is stored securely on your phone.
  • With Premium: through our server (api.ateright.com) to Anthropic. Our server passes the request along and returns the answer; it does not keep the content. For the chat feature, the request includes a summary of that day's log so the answers fit your day; weekly insights and the written read in Premium reports include a summary of that period (averages, days within the calorie goal, macros, most-logged foods, weight trend, and goal). The report numbers themselves are calculated on your phone.

Before anything is sent to an AI service for the first time, the app shows who will receive it and what is sent, and sends nothing unless you tap Allow. You can withdraw that permission anytime in Settings > AI data sharing; AI features then pause until you allow them again.

Food lookups may also query the USDA FoodData Central database and Open Food Facts (for barcodes) with the food name or barcode number only.

Reporting an AI answer

AI answers (chat replies, recipe notes and ideas, weekly insights, the written read in reports) have a flag button. If you use it, the app sends that answer's text, the reason you picked, your optional note, and which AI service wrote it to our server so we can review it. A report contains no license key, name, or diary data. We use reports only to find and fix harmful or wrong answers, and delete them after 12 months.

Bug reports and ideas

You can report a bug or suggest an improvement from the app (Settings) or on our support page. We receive only what is in the form: your message, your email address if you choose to give one (used only to reply to you), and, from the app, the app version and phone type. A bug report from the app can also include the app's troubleshooting log if you leave that box ticked; it lists what the app was doing (AI calls and timings, errors, and food names it looked up), with any keys removed before sending, and never includes your food diary. We use these only to fix problems and improve AteRight, and delete them after two years, or sooner on request.

Reminders and progress

Good Weeks, milestones, and the optional reminders are worked out and scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Steps and health data

If you turn on step reading, AteRight reads your daily step count from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone). Steps are read only, used on your phone, and never sent anywhere or written back.

Premium and payments

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple; we never see your card details. To run Premium, our server keeps a license record: the license key, its status, your monthly AI usage count, and, for store purchases, a reference to the purchase so we can confirm it is still active. This record contains no name, email, or food data.

Children

AteRight is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app. To have a Premium license record, or reports and messages you sent us, removed, follow the steps on our data deletion page. Questions about this policy: admin@cardrama.gg.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
