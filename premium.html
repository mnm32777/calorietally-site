How to get it

Subscribe inside the app

Install AteRight

From Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.

Open Settings, then "AteRight Premium"

The gear at the top right of any screen. You can also tap any locked report.

Pick monthly or yearly

Payment goes through Google Play. Cancel anytime in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions. New phone? Tap "Restore purchase".

The fine print, plainly: one subscription covers one person; there is a generous monthly allowance of 600 AI calls, about ten times what a heavy user needs; your food diary still never leaves your phone; and cancelling simply ends Premium. Your Good Weeks and milestones stay free, and your own free keys keep working regardless.
