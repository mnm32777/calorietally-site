Data deletion

Delete your AteRight data

AteRight is made by Cardrama, LLC. There are no AteRight accounts, so almost everything lives on your own phone and is yours to delete. Here is every place your data can be, and how to remove it.

Delete everything on your phone

Your food log, saved foods, recipes, weigh-ins, goals, settings, and saved AI keys are stored only on your device. To delete all of it, uninstall AteRight. To delete it but keep the app installed: Android Settings > Apps > AteRight > Storage > Clear data (on iPhone, delete and reinstall the app). This is immediate and permanent.

Cancel Premium first (if you subscribe)

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Google Play (Play Store > profile > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions) or on iPhone (Settings > your name > Subscriptions).

Delete your Premium license record

If you used Premium, our server keeps a small license record: your license key, whether it is active, your monthly AI usage count, and a reference to your store purchase. It contains no name, email, or food data. To have it deleted, email admin@cardrama.gg with the subject "AteRight data deletion" and include your Google Play or Apple order number (it is in your purchase receipt email), or your license key if you entered one by hand. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: everything on your phone (on uninstall or clearing data), and your Premium license record (on request).
  • Never stored by us, so nothing to delete: meal descriptions, photos, recipes, and chat messages you send to AI features. They are processed to answer your request and not kept by AteRight.
  • Kept by others: Google Play and Apple keep their own records of purchases under their policies. If you use your own AI key, that request also goes to the provider you chose (Groq, Google, or Anthropic) under your account and their retention policy.
  • Retention: we keep nothing beyond the license record, and we delete that within 30 days of your request.
