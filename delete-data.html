Data deletion

Delete your AteRight data

AteRight is made by CardRama, LLC. There are no AteRight accounts, so almost everything lives on your own phone and is yours to delete. Here is every place your data can be, and how to remove it.

Delete everything on your phone

Your food log, saved foods, recipes, weigh-ins, goals, settings, saved AI keys, and your free lookup pass code are stored only on your device. To delete all of it, uninstall AteRight. To delete it but keep the app installed: Android Settings > Apps > AteRight > Storage > Clear data (on iPhone, delete and reinstall the app). This is immediate and permanent.

Cancel Premium first (if you subscribe)

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Google Play (Play Store > profile > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions) or on iPhone (Settings > your name > Subscriptions).

Delete your Premium license record

If you used Premium, our server keeps a small license record: your license key, whether it is active, your monthly AI usage count, and a reference to your store purchase. It contains no name, email, or food data. To have it deleted, email admin@cardrama.gg with the subject "AteRight data deletion" and include your license key, which only your phone shows: open AteRight, go to Settings > AteRight Premium, and copy the key from there. The key is what proves the record is yours; a receipt or order number can be seen by other people, so we do not act on those alone. If you no longer have the app, reinstall it and tap Restore purchase to see the key, then uninstall again. We delete the record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: everything on your phone (on uninstall or clearing data), and on request your Premium license record and any bug reports, ideas, or AI-answer reports you sent us (tell us roughly when, and the email you used if you gave one).
  • Never stored by us, so nothing to delete: meal descriptions, photos, recipes, and chat messages you send to AI features. They are processed to answer your request and not kept by AteRight.
  • Kept by others: Google Play and Apple keep their own records of purchases under their policies. If you use your own AI key, that request also goes to the provider you chose (Groq, Google, or Anthropic) under your account and their retention policy.
  • Free AI lookup pass: if you used free AI lookups, our server keeps a small pass record: the pass code, when it was made, its limits, how many AI calls it has made, and when its first call was. It holds no name, email, device ID, or food data, so nothing in it identifies you. It deletes itself about 13 months after it was made. Uninstalling the app removes the pass code from your phone. Our server also counts how many passes each internet connection asks for in a day, under a scrambled form of the IP address block (never the address itself), and deletes that count 2 days later.
  • Retention: we keep only the license record, the free lookup pass record (about 13 months, then it deletes itself), AI-answer reports (12 months), and bug reports and ideas you chose to send (2 years). We delete the license record, reports, and messages within 30 days of your request.
