Quick fixes

Before you report

The app says "Add a free AI key first"

The free app needs an AI key to understand your food. Settings, then "How to get your free keys" walks you through it in about two minutes. Or choose Premium and skip keys.

The AI gives an error

Settings, AI provider, then "Test key". The test explains what is wrong, and if your provider retired a model it switches you to a current one.

Photos do not work

Tap "Test key" once in Settings so the app can find your provider's photo-capable model. A free Gemini key always works for photos.

A barcode is not found

Not every product is in the free database. Type the food instead, or photograph the Nutrition Facts label with the camera's Label mode.

Reminders never show up

Check that notifications are allowed for AteRight in your phone's settings, then turn the reminder off and on again in Settings, Reminders.

I want my data deleted

Your food diary lives only on your phone; uninstalling removes it. For anything AteRight holds (a Premium license, reports you sent), see delete your data.
