Your diary is yours. Not ours. Not anyone's.

We can't sell what we don't have

Four things we can't do with your data

🚫

We can't sell it

Your meals, weigh-ins, recipes and goals are stored only on your phone. There is no AteRight copy to sell, rent or trade.

👤

We can't profile you

There is no account. No name, no email, no password, nothing to tie a diary to a person.

📵

We can't track you

No ads, no ad software, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites. Apple's label for AteRight says it: Data Used to Track You, none.

🔒

We can't leak it

We keep no database of food diaries, so there is nothing of yours on our side for anyone to break into.

Why it matters

A food diary says more than you think

What you eat, when you eat and how your weight moves can point to a health condition, a pregnancy, a diet or a medicine. Some of the biggest calorie apps say so in their own privacy policies, and still share data with advertising partners. Once data is shared for ads, you can't see where it goes next.

AteRight was built the other way around from day one. See the side-by-side comparison.

The honest part

The one thing that leaves your phone, and only when you ask

To work out the calories in "turkey sandwich and an apple," or in a photo of your plate, AteRight sends the words or the photo of that one lookup to an AI service. AteRight never uploads your diary. (If you use Premium's chat or weekly insights, that request also carries a short summary, such as the day's totals, so the answer fits your day. Our server doesn't keep it.)

It asks first

Before anything goes out the first time, the app names the AI service and what is sent, and sends nothing unless you tap Allow. You can take that back anytime.

We pass it on, we don't keep it

Free lookups and Premium go through our server to Anthropic, which does not train its AI on them. Our server keeps none of it.

Your own key, your choice

With your own free key, the lookup goes straight to the service you picked, under its own terms. Google's free Gemini tier may use requests to improve its products; Groq or Premium are there if you'd rather avoid that.

Our promise

  • We will never sell your data.
  • We will never show you ads.
  • We will never add tracking.
  • Your diary stays on your phone. We never get a copy.
