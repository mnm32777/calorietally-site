The full tour

Every feature, almost all of them free

Everything on this page is in the free app, except the few marked Premium. The free app runs on your own free AI key; Premium runs on ours and adds reports and a chat companion. Compare them here.

🍽️ Four ways to log a meal

⌨️

Type it

Write your meal like a text message and tap Look up. The AI splits it into foods with portions, calories, protein, carbs, and fat, and asks at most one short question when a detail really matters, like "fried or grilled?"

🎤

Say it

Tap the mic and talk. Your words stream into the box live, and you can pause to think as long as you like. Great while cooking with messy hands.

📷

Photograph it

Take a photo of your plate and the app answers: "Chicken taco with sauce, about 280. Rice, about 210. Look right?" Leave your fork in the shot and portion guesses get sharper. You confirm before anything is saved.

📦

Scan it

Barcodes resolve through the free Open Food Facts database. No barcode? Photograph the Nutrition Facts label instead and the printed numbers are read exactly.

Extra camera trick: Leftovers mode. Photo before, photo after, and only what you actually ate gets logged.

🧠 A food memory that works for you

Every confirmed food joins your personal library with its portion and numbers. Repeats become one-tap Quick log buttons. Portions are asked in human units: 1 chicken breast, 2 tacos, half a serving, with live calories as you adjust. Logged something wrong? Tap to fix the amount or move it to another meal, swipe to delete with a 5-second Undo. Mistakes cost nothing here.

🏪 Restaurant menus, saved

📸

Scan once

Photograph a menu, one photo per page. Every dish is read with estimated nutrition and kept in the menu's own sections. Calories printed on the menu are used as printed.

🗂️

The Menus tab

Every menu is saved under the restaurant's name. Search it, star your regular places so they stay on top, rescan when the menu changes. Same restaurant spelled a little differently? The app notices and asks.

⭐

Picks for right now

Stars mark the dishes that fit the calories and protein you have left today, recalculated every time you open the menu. With Diabetes support on, picks favor dishes gentler on blood sugar.

🍳

Make it at home

Tap a dish and get a similar recipe to cook yourself, with ingredients, steps, and real nutrition for your version. It is not the restaurant's own recipe, and it says so; it is a close home take you can save and tweak.

📖 Recipes with a brain

🧮

Analyze anything

List the ingredients and the AI returns per-serving calories and macros plus a 1 to 10 health score with friendly notes that always start with what is good about the dish.

🏷️

Exact mode

Scan the barcode of each ingredient and say how much goes in the pot. Scanned items use real label data the AI is not allowed to second-guess.

✏️

Edit freely

Change quantities or ingredients anytime. The app asks: replace the original, or keep both versions?

💌

Share with friends

Send a recipe as a tidy text card. Friends with AteRight paste it in and get the full recipe, numbers and all.

📚

Scan a recipe Premium

Point the camera at a cookbook page, a recipe card, or handwriting, even across several pages. It arrives typed up and ready to analyze.

🍲 The variety engine. Eating the same five safe meals is how diets die of boredom. The "New ideas" tab reads what you actually eat and suggests recipes that feel familiar but fresh, sized to your calorie goal, each with a "why you might like this" pitch. One tap turns an idea into an analyzed recipe in your book.

🏅 Progress that rewards showing up Free

No streaks to break. Progress here is about showing up, never about the number: a day over your calorie goal counts exactly like any other, and nothing looks at your calories or weight.

👋

Welcome back

Took a few days off? Your first log back earns a badge, because coming back is the part that matters.

🔔

Gentle reminders

Both optional and off until you turn them on: a Monday 8 am recap of last week, and a 7:30 pm nudge that only appears on a day with nothing logged. Made on your phone.

🎯 Goals that fit your life

⚖️

Lose, maintain, or gain

Pick a direction and a pace. Your daily calorie goal comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the standard clinical formula, with safety floors it never crosses.

📈

Self-correcting

Formulas are starting guesses. After a few weekly weigh-ins the app compares your real trend to the plan and suggests a small tweak to your calorie goal. Nothing changes unless you tap yes.

🥩

Macro goals

Protein, carb, and fat targets in grams with sensible defaults and full custom control. Protein-forward, because muscle is worth keeping.

👟

Step aware

Reads daily steps from Health Connect. Optionally, steps beyond 5,000 earn a few honest calories back, because your calorie goal already covers normal moving around.

📊 Reports Premium

Made on your phone from the diary you already keep. Only the short written "AteRight's read" uses the AI.

💚 Diabetes support

An optional mode for people who manage diabetes. Carbs get top billing on every meal and food. Each food gets a plain-words flag: steady (green), moderate (orange), or fast carbs (red), with a one-line reason like "white bun hits fast" or "fiber and protein keep this steady". Recipes gain a blood sugar note with one gentle swap, and menu picks favor options that are easier on blood sugar. With Premium, the Carbs and blood sugar report shows carbs at each meal day by day, how steady each meal is, and your steady, moderate and fast mix.

This is information to help you think about food. It is not medical advice, and it never replaces your care team's guidance on medication, insulin, or treatment.

🔒 Private by design

📱

Your phone, your data

No account, no cloud, no analytics, nothing to sell. Every log lives in a database on your device, with one-tap backup and restore (menus and milestones included).

🔑

Your own AI key

The free app runs on a key you create with Google or Groq (Anthropic works too). Guided setup in the app. Keys are kept in your phone's secure storage. If one provider has a bad day, the app retries with your other keys.

✋

You decide

Before anything goes to an AI service, the app shows who receives it and what is sent, and waits for your yes. Change your mind anytime in Settings.

🛠️

Fixable

Report a bug or suggest an idea right from the app or this website. A troubleshooting log can ride along with a bug report, never your keys or your diary.

🎨 Make it yours. 20 color themes, each in light and dark, or follow your phone's setting.
