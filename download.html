Before you start

What you will need

📱

An Android phone or iPhone

Android 8 or newer, or iOS 17 or newer. Camera for the photo and barcode features, microphone for voice logging. Both optional.

🔑

No key to start

You get 35 free AI lookups, with no key and no card. To keep going free after that, add a free key from Google (aistudio.google.com) or Groq (console.groq.com). Two minutes, no credit card. The app walks you through it step by step.

⏱️

Five minutes

Install, answer a few quick questions about yourself, and log your first meal with a free lookup.

AteRight will only ever be offered through Google Play and the App Store. We never send the app by email or message, and we never ask for passwords, payment details, or your AI key anywhere outside the app on your own phone.

Stuck? The app can explain itself

Settings has a full plain-language Help section, guided key setup, and a troubleshooting log.
