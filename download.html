Before you start

What you will need

📱

An Android phone

Android 8 or newer (iPhone coming soon). Camera for the photo and barcode features, microphone for voice logging. Both optional.

🔑

One free AI key

From Google (aistudio.google.com) or Groq (console.groq.com). Free, two minutes, no credit card. The app walks you through it step by step.

⏱️

Five minutes

Install, answer a few quick questions about yourself, grab the key, and log your first meal.

AteRight will only ever be offered through Google Play and the App Store. We never send the app by email or message, and we never ask for passwords, payment details, or your AI key anywhere outside the app on your own phone.

Stuck? The app can explain itself

Settings has a full plain-language Help section, guided key setup, and a troubleshooting log.
